04 / 07
Craft — 20% of the score, and the whole Reve track

We made it behave like aerosol, not like a sticker.

  • Feathered noise edges — overspray, not a crisp alpha cut
  • Colour pulled toward the wall so pigment sits in the surface
  • Grazing-incidence thinning — paint thins as the wall turns away
  • Multi-coat variation and a Sobel wall-grain normal map, so brick and concrete read through the paint

Reve is the design backbone, not a filter: one hand-tuned house style drives the artwork, the map pins and the charts. Applied to remote pieces too, so a stranger's tag looks painted on your wall rather than pasted over your camera.
